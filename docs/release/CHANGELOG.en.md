# What's New

## 1.1.0 — In review

- Open Settings anytime, even during rest. A new default rest applies from your next rest.
- Refreshed screens: press and hold End (top left) to finish a workout, and swipe the preset row to pick 10s–5min.
- iOS 26 or later: rest end rings as an alarm, even in Silent mode or a Focus. Tap "+30 sec" to rest a little longer.
- Rest-end alerts are now Time Sensitive. Settings tells you when they are off.
- New app icon (Light, Dark, Tinted) and Lock Screen widget.
- Now runs on iOS and iPadOS 18 and watchOS 11 or later.

## 1.0 — September 27, 2026

First release.

- Set rest and countdown modes, quick presets, pause and resume.
- Final 3-second sound and haptics, rest-end alerts.
- Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, Home and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center control.
- Apple Watch app.
- Your last 30 workout summaries, stored on device.
- Korean and English.
