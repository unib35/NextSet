# NextSet Support

Can’t find your answer below? Send us an email.

## Timer

### Changing the rest duration

Swipe the preset row on the timer screen to pick 10s–5min, or change Default rest in Settings (gear).

You can open Settings during rest too; a new default applies from your next rest.

### Ending a workout

Press and hold End at the top left for about a second. A short tap does nothing, so you won’t end by accident.

With VoiceOver, a confirmation appears instead.

## Alerts

### Missing sounds or notifications

Check the app’s Sound option and your device’s notification permissions and volume.

> **iOS 26 or later** Rings as an alarm — Allow alarms at your first rest and rest end rings as an alarm, even in Silent mode or a Focus. Tap “+30 sec” to rest a little longer. If you declined, turn it back on in iPhone Settings → Apps → NextSet.

> **iOS 18 · alarms off** Arrives as a notification — Rest end uses a notification, which only vibrates in Silent mode. To get it during a Focus, turn on Settings → Notifications → NextSet → Time Sensitive Notifications. The app’s Advanced settings show a notice when it is off.

### Alerts and Apple Watch

The device where you start a rest schedules its end alert. Allow notifications on Apple Watch for rests started there. Alerts for rests started on iPhone follow the system’s Apple Watch notification routing settings.

Changes made on another device require a connection. While disconnected, also change or stop the timer on the device that scheduled the alert.

### No final 3-second sound outside the app

The final 3-second sound and haptic cues play while the app is open. Outside the app, a system notification tells you rest has ended.

### An alert couldn’t be scheduled

If the app reports that an alert could not be scheduled, tap Try again. If it still fails, keep the app open until rest ends and check your notification settings.

## Lock Screen & widgets

### Seeing the timer with the screen off

Enable Live Activity in Settings to show the timer on supported Lock Screens and Dynamic Island. The system may delay the transition to the rest-ended display.

### Adding widgets

Open Settings (gear) → All settings → How to add widgets.

### Switching to the Dark or Tinted icon

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen → Edit → Customize, then choose Dark or Tinted.

## Records

### Where are workout records stored?

The app keeps your last 30 workout summaries locally. Paired devices exchange summaries when connected. The developer does not receive these records.

### Deleting workout records

Delete records using the trash button in Recent workouts. Deletion applies to that device, so delete records separately on any other paired device.
